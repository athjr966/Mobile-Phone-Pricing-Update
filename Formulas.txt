Main formulas used

Revenue After Fee:
=Promo Price × (1 - Platform Fee %) 
 
Profit
=Revenue After Fee - Cost Price

Margin %
=Profit / Revenue After Fee

vsCompetitor
=Promo Price - Competitor Price

Status
=IF(Profit < 0, "LOSS RISK", IF(Margin < 0.05, "Tight Margin", "OK"))

Risk Level
=IF(Status = "LOSS RISK", "CRITICAL", IF(Margin < 0.02, "High Risk", "Medium"))

Working Sheet:

Cost Price (VLOOKUP)
=VLOOKUP(Product Code, Cleaned data range, 5, FALSE)


KPI Summary Formulas:

Total Products
=COUNTA(Working sheet range) - 1

Highest Margin
=MAX(Margin % range)

Average Margin
=AVERAGE(Margin % range)

Lowest Margin
=MIN(Margin % range)

Products at Risk (<5%)
=COUNTIF(Margin % range, "<0.05")

Loss Risk Products
=COUNTIF(Status range, "LOSS RISK")
